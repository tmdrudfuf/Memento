# Google Play listing — English (en-US)

**App name** (≤30): `Memento: Memory Boards`

**Short description** (≤80): `Pin one photo, keep the whole memory. Private boards for the moments you love.`

**Full description** (≤4000):

```
Your camera roll has thousands of photos. The moments you want to relive are buried somewhere in there.

Memento keeps only the ones you choose.

ONE PHOTO OPENS THE WHOLE MEMORY
Pick one photo that brings a moment back: the ramen you had in Tokyo, a train ticket, the dinner table on Mom's birthday. That photo becomes the cover of a memory. Inside it, keep the related photos, a short video, the date and a line to remember it by.

PIN IT TO A BOARD
Memories are pinned to boards like "Japan 2026", "Us", "Family" or "College". Over the years, your wall of boards becomes a collection of your life, not a folder of files.

SAVE A MOMENT IN 3 TAPS
Tap +, pick a photo, tap a board. Done. No typing, no forms. Add details later, whenever you like.

REDISCOVER
• "Remember this?" brings back a memory you haven't opened in a while
• See what you pinned on this day in past years (Premium)
• Look back on your year with a yearly recap built from the photos you chose (Premium)
• An optional gentle Sunday reminder

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your memories stay on your phone. No account needed.
• Memento only sees the photos you pick, never your whole library.
• Back up everything to one file and restore it on a new phone.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Memento is free to use with up to 3 boards and unlimited memories, with a small ad around the collection (never inside a memory).
Memento Premium adds unlimited boards, no ads, "On this day" and the yearly recap. Monthly or yearly subscription; a Founder's Lifetime option is available for early supporters.
If Premium ends, every memory stays yours and fully accessible.

Dark mode · English and Korean

We're not collecting photos. We're collecting memories.
```
