Memento — Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Short version: Memento keeps everything on your device. We don't collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data.

What the app stores

When you save a memory, Memento keeps a copy of the photos and videos you chose, plus any title, note and date you add. This is stored only inside the app's private storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Memento has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. Your photos, videos and notes are never uploaded by the app.

Permissions

  • Photos: Memento uses the system photo picker, so it can only see the specific photos and videos you choose. It does not read your whole library.
  • Camera and microphone: requested only when you tap Camera, to take a photo or video for a memory.

Backups

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android) may include the app's data, according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting a memory or a jar in the app removes its copies from the app. Uninstalling Memento deletes all of its data from your device. Your original photos in your Photos library are never changed or deleted by Memento.

Children

Memento does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
