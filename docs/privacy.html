Memento — Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Short version: your memories stay on your device. We never see your photos, videos or notes. The free version shows a small, non-personalized ad on two screens; Premium has no ads.

What the app stores

When you save a memory, Memento keeps a copy of the photos and videos you chose, plus any title, note and date you add. This is stored only inside the app's private storage on your device. Memento has no accounts and no server of its own, so your memories are never uploaded by us.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the Home and board screens, provided by Google AdMob. Ads never appear while you capture or view a memory, and ad providers never receive your photos, videos or notes.

  • Memento requests non-personalized ads only: ads are not based on your interests or browsing history.
  • Google AdMob may still collect limited device data (such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data) for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, as described in Google's advertising policies.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Memento asks for your consent choices (via Google's consent tool) before any ad is requested. You can change them later in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Upgrading to Premium removes ads and the ad SDK stops requesting ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. Memento uses RevenueCat to confirm which plan you have. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts, never your memories. We don't receive your payment card details.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to a server.

Permissions

  • Photos: Memento uses the system photo picker, so it can only see the specific photos and videos you choose. It does not read your whole library.
  • Camera and microphone: requested only when you tap Camera, to take a photo or video for a memory.
  • Notifications: requested only if you turn on the weekly reminder.

Backups

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android) may include the app's data, according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and your platform provider, not by us.

Deleting your data

Deleting a memory or a board in the app removes its copies from the app. Settings → Delete all data removes everything. Uninstalling Memento deletes all of its data from your device. Your original photos in your Photos library are never changed or deleted by Memento.

Children

Memento is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
