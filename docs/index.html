Memento

One photo opens the whole memory.

Your camera roll has thousands of photos. Memento keeps only the moments you choose. Pick one photo that brings a moment back, like a meal, a ticket or a view. That photo becomes the cover of a memory, and inside it you keep the related photos, videos and a short note. Memories collect in jars: Japan 2026, Us, Family.

  • Save a memory in 3 taps. No typing required.
  • Private by design: your memories stay on your phone. No account needed.
  • Free to use, with a small ad around the collection (never inside a memory). Premium removes ads and adds unlimited jars, rediscovery and a yearly recap.
  • Sees only the photos you pick.
